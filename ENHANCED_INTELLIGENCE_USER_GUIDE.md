# Enhanced Intelligence Features - User Guide

## 🎯 Overview

The Next Career Intelligence platform now includes advanced predictive analytics and market intelligence features powered by a suite of 10 specialized AI agents. These enhancements transform the platform into a comprehensive career intelligence powerhouse.

---

## 🆕 New Features

### 1. Career Radar Dashboard

**What it does:** Real-time career health monitoring with threat detection and peer benchmarking.

**Key Components:**
- **Risk Score** (0-100): Overall career stability assessment
- **Active Threats**: Issues requiring immediate attention
- **Peer Ranking**: Your percentile vs industry peers
- **Priority Actions**: AI-recommended next steps

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to Dashboard → Career Radar
2. Review your risk score and threat levels
3. Check peer benchmarking results
4. Take action on priority recommendations

**Subscription Requirements:**
- Risk Overview: Free tier
- Full Risk Scan: Pro tier ($29/mo)
- Advanced Threats: Elite tier ($99/mo)

---

### 2. Career Path Visualizer

**What it does:** Interactive visualization of your predicted career trajectories over 3-5 years.

**Features:**
- **Predicted Roles**: Future positions with probability scores
- **Salary Forecasts**: Expected compensation ranges
- **Skill Evolution**: Year-by-year skill development plan
- **Alternative Paths**: Multiple career direction options

**How to use:**
1. Go to Dashboard → Career Forecast
2. Select time horizon (1-5 years)
3. Review predicted career paths
4. Explore skill evolution timeline
5. Consider alternative trajectories

**Subscription Requirements:**
- 1-year forecast: Free tier
- 3-year forecast: Pro tier
- 5-year forecast: Elite tier

---

### 3. Market Intelligence

**What it does:** Real-time market data, salary trends, and emerging skills analysis.

**Three Views:**

**Overview Tab:**
- Market demand levels (high/medium/low)
- Salary trend direction
- Competition metrics
- Key market insights

**Salary Tab:**
- Current salary ranges
- Percentile distribution (25th, 50th, 75th, 90th)
- Trend analysis
- Location-adjusted data

**Skills Tab:**
- Hot skills in-demand
- Emerging skills with growth rates
- Adoption stage indicators
- Related role mappings

**How to use:**
1. Enter your role or target role
2. Optionally specify location
3. Browse the three tabs for insights
4. Identify skill gaps and opportunities

**Subscription Requirements:**
- Basic market data: Free tier
- Full salary breakdown: Pro tier
- Emerging skills: Elite tier

---

### 4. Job Offer Analyzer

**What it does:** Comprehensive analysis of job offers with negotiation intelligence.

**Analysis Includes:**
- **Total Compensation**: Year 1 and 4-year projections
- **Market Comparison**: Percentile ranking vs market
- **Negotiation Strategy**: Leverage assessment and talking points
- **Benefits Valuation**: Dollar value of benefits package
- **Recommendations**: Accept, negotiate, or pass guidance

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to Jobs → Offer Analyzer
2. Input offer details:
   - Base salary
   - Bonus structure
   - Equity (RSUs, options)
   - Benefits (health, 401k, PTO)
   - Remote policy
3. Review comprehensive analysis
4. Use negotiation recommendations

**Subscription Requirements:**
- Basic analysis: Pro tier
- Advanced negotiation: Elite tier
- Counteroffer generator: Elite tier

---

### 5. Early Warning System

**What it does:** Proactive threat detection for your career stability.

**Monitors:**
- **Skill Obsolescence**: Technologies becoming outdated
- **Industry Instability**: Sector-wide risks
- **Automation Risk**: AI displacement probability
- **Market Shifts**: Demand changes for your role

**Alert Levels:**
- 🔴 Critical: Immediate action required
- 🟠 High: Address within 3 months
- 🟡 Medium: Monitor and plan
- 🟢 Low: Informational only

**How to use:**
1. Dashboard automatically shows active threats
2. Review threat details and severity
3. Follow recommended actions
4. Set up weekly digest notifications (Elite)

**Subscription Requirements:**
- Basic threats: Pro tier
- Advanced scanning: Elite tier
- Custom alerts: Enterprise

---

### 6. Peer Benchmarking

**What it does:** Compare your career progress against similar professionals.

**Benchmarks:**
- **Salary Percentile**: How your compensation ranks
- **Skills Assessment**: Breadth and quality vs peers
- **Progression Speed**: Career advancement pace
- **Overall Rating**: Exceptional/Above Average/Average/Below

**Peer Cohort Defined By:**
- Role and seniority level
- Industry and location
- Years of experience
- Similar career stage

**How to use:**
1. Ensure profile is complete for accurate matching
2. View benchmark report in Career Radar
3. Identify your strengths (areas excelling)
4. Address improvement areas
5. Track percentile changes over time

**Subscription Requirements:**
- Basic benchmark: Pro tier
- Full breakdown: Elite tier
- Historical tracking: Elite tier

## 🎓 Best Practices

### 1. Keep Your Profile Updated
- Add all relevant skills (technical + soft)
- Update job titles and experience
- Set clear career goals
- Specify preferences (remote, salary, etc.)

**Why:** More complete profiles = more accurate predictions

### 2. Check Risk Scan Weekly
- Review new threats
- Track risk score trends
- Act on critical alerts promptly

**Why:** Early detection prevents career setbacks

### 3. Use Market Intel for Job Search
- Compare multiple roles' market data
- Validate salary expectations
- Identify emerging skills to learn
- Time your job moves strategically

**Why:** Data-driven decisions beat gut instinct

### 4. Leverage Offer Analyzer for Negotiations
- Input multiple offers to compare
- Use negotiation talking points
- Understand total comp beyond base salary
- Know your market percentile

**Why:** Better negotiations = higher compensation

### 5. Plan Around Career Forecast
- Align skill learning with trajectory
- Hit key milestones proactively
- Prepare for inflection points
- Consider alternative paths

**Why:** Intentional career planning accelerates growth

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Getting the Most Value

**For Job Seekers:**
1. Run market snapshot for target roles before applying
2. Use peer benchmark to set realistic salary expectations
3. Check risk scan to avoid declining industries
4. Analyze offers comprehensively, not just base salary

**For Career Planners:**
1. Review 3-year forecast quarterly
2. Build skills from "year 1" evolution plan first
3. Track progression timing for target roles
4. Set calendar reminders for key milestones

**For Negotiators:**
1. Run offer analysis before accepting any offer
2. Use market data to justify counteroffer
3. Value benefits holistically (not just salary)
4. Know your leverage level (low/medium/high)

**For Career Health:**
1. Enable weekly risk digest emails (Elite)
2. Address "high" threats within 1 month
3. Benchmark quarterly to track progress
4. Build buffers before making big moves

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "No forecast available"
**Solution:** Complete your profile with skills, goals, and experience

### "Insufficient data for benchmark"
**Solution:** Ensure role, industry, and seniority level are set

### "Market data unavailable"
**Solution:** Try alternative role title or remove location filter

### Risk score seems high
**Solution:** This is intentional - we're conservative to keep you safe. Address threats to lower it.
